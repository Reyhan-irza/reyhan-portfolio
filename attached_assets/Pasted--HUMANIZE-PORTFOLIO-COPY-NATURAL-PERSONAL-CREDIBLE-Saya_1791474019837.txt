==================================================
3. HUMAN WRITING RULE
==================================================

Tulis seperti seseorang yang benar-benar mengalami proses tersebut.

Lebih baik:

"I built this because..."

daripada:

"This project demonstrates..."

Lebih baik:

"I started with..."

daripada:

"This initiative represents..."

Lebih baik:

"I’m still learning..."

daripada:

"I continuously pursue professional growth..."

Lebih baik:

"I ran into problems, fixed them, and kept going."

daripada:

"The project underwent iterative refinement."

Lebih baik:

"I wanted to see if I could build..."

daripada:

"This project explores the implementation of..."
