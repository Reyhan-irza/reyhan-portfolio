---
name: Portfolio story privacy
description: Language and privacy boundaries for first-person portfolio storytelling.
---

Use warm, respectful, polished Indonesian in first person with “saya.” Start the visitor experience with a greeting. Keep natural English industry terms, brand and technology names, official titles, and project names when translation would feel forced. Keep personal online activities unspecified; do not infer or invent motivations, biographical details, dates, or outcomes.

**Why:** The user asked for an adult-appropriate, courteous voice that is still welcoming across ages, with a greeting and without forced translation or private activity details.

**How to apply:** When revising portfolio copy, use “saya,” greet visitors early, preserve only confirmed details, and keep unnamed activities broad.
