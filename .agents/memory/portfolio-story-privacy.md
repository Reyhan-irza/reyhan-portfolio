---
name: Portfolio story privacy
description: Language and privacy boundaries for first-person portfolio storytelling.
---

Use natural, polished informal Indonesian in first person with “gue.” Keep personal online activities unspecified unless the user explicitly approves details; do not infer or invent motivations, biographical details, dates, or outcomes.

**Why:** The user asked for a stronger personal story based only on confirmed facts while keeping private online activities non-explicit.

**How to apply:** When revising the portfolio's story, preserve its first-person voice, use only confirmed details, and keep unnamed activities broad.
