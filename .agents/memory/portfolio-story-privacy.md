---
name: Portfolio story privacy
description: Language and privacy boundaries for first-person portfolio storytelling.
---

Use warm, respectful, engaging Indonesian in first person with “saya” only for personal storytelling (About/origin and relevant journey descriptions) and natural visitor communication (comments, replies, contact microcopy, and visitor messages). Storytelling should feel friendly, with distinctive but readable typography and purposeful, non-generic scroll motion. Keep the rest of the portfolio UI, navigation, headings, metadata, and project descriptions in English. Preserve natural industry terms, brands, technology names, official titles, and project names. Keep personal online activities unspecified; do not infer or invent motivations, biographical details, dates, or outcomes.

**Why:** The user asked to localize storytelling and visitor communication only, keeping the existing premium English UI and a courteous voice across ages; they also said the existing story felt plain, the font boring, and ScrollTrigger too standard.

**How to apply:** Keep Indonesian confined to the approved narrative and visitor-message surfaces; preserve the English visual and interface system, while making story typography and motion expressive without inventing personal facts or compromising readability.
